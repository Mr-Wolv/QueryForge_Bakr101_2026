# Community standards

QueryForge welcomes respectful, constructive participation from people with different backgrounds
and experience levels.

Expected behavior includes:

- Treating others with respect and discussing ideas without personal attacks.
- Giving and receiving technical feedback constructively.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report concerns
privately to a maintainer through GitHub rather than in a public issue. Maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
